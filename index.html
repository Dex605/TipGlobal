About Tip Global PTE.LTD

Headquartered in Singapore, Tip Global PTE.LTD is an integrated global brand promotion platform that connects businesses with powerful advertising campaigns across digital, broadcast, and out-of-home channels. As a Private Limited company registered under Singapore law, we operate across 60 countries with a network of thousands of active brand promoters.

By leveraging both qualitative and quantitative consumer insights, we implement advanced brand strategies that generate exposure, drive engagement, and enhance visibility for partner brands worldwide. Our solution is specifically designed for consumer brands, luxury houses, technology companies, and financial institutions.

We provide comprehensive brand promotion solutions encompassing campaign management, audience targeting, promoter networks, and performance analytics — eliminating the need for fragmented tools and streamlining execution.

Why Singapore?

Singapore's position as Asia's premier financial and business hub makes it the ideal home for a global brand promotion platform. Our Singapore incorporation allows us to operate with transparency, regulatory compliance, and the trust of international partner brands across every major market.

Tip Global Advertising Guidelines
1. Depositing Campaign Funds ▼
Before allocating funds to any brand campaign, clients are advised to contact our online support team to verify and confirm the correct campaign account details. After making a deposit, clients must provide the payment receipt to our support team to ensure that their accounts and campaign budgets are updated promptly.
2. Brand Promotion Campaigns ▼
The performance and reach of each brand promotion campaign may vary depending on market conditions, target audiences, and seasonal trends. Campaigns are allocated based on the available promotion budget in each client account.
3. Withdrawal of Campaign Funds ▼
Clients may request withdrawal of remaining campaign funds at any time. Standard support hours for processing fund adjustments are from 9:59 AM to 10:00 PM Eastern Time (USA).
4. Referral and Partner Program ▼
Clients and promotion partners can invite other brands, agencies, or individuals to join Tip Global. Referring new partners earns a 20% referral commission based on the profits generated by their initial campaigns. Partners may also use referral codes to create a network of sub-agencies, generating a downline.
5. Operating Hours ▼
Clients may manage and launch brand promotion campaigns during daily operating hours from 8:59 AM to 9:00 PM Eastern Time (USA).
Support ▼
For clarification or assistance regarding campaign setup, budget management, or account issues, please click "Contact Us" on the platform to reach our online support team.
